    About Recovery Quest

    Mohamed Ayadi

    Founder & Developer

    Recovery Quest is an AI-powered health and wellness platform designed to help users understand and improve their overall well-being. The platform combines health tracking, fitness monitoring, nutrition insights, and intelligent recommendations into a single experience.

    Users can monitor important health metrics, review progress through interactive dashboards, receive personalized guidance, and access AI-assisted tools that support healthier lifestyle decisions.

    The long-term vision of Recovery Quest is to make preventive healthcare more accessible by transforming health data into clear, actionable insights that help users build better habits and achieve their wellness goals.
